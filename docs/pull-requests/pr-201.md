# [PR 201](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/201) feat(mesh): M1a, analytic edge polylines

Fixes #142

## What
- **`stepv::mesh::edges`** (new `src/mesh/`): `Polylines::build(&g, edges, mm_per_unit, Tolerance)` turns every `LINE`/`CIRCLE`/`ELLIPSE` `EDGE_CURVE` (directly or through `SURFACE_CURVE`/`SEAM_CURVE`) into one canonical polyline, from vertex point to vertex point, built once into a struct-of-arrays buffer. `oriented(&g, oriented_edge)` walks it in the oriented edge's sense as `(EdgePoint { edge, index }, point)`.
- **The §2.6 bounds, as written:** circles `n = max(n_min, ⌈max(θ/a, θ/(2·acos(1 − min(d/r, 1))))⌉)`; ellipses `Δt ≤ min(a·B/A, √(8d/A))`; `n_min` 1 open, 3 closed. The sweep follows `same_sense` through the 0/2π seam.
- **Routed, never dropped:** other curves (`unsupported curve`), a zero semi-axis (`degenerate conic`), more than 2^20 segments (`too many segments`, so a needle ellipse can't exhaust memory).
- **Shared curve resolver:** `src/graph/curve.rs` now holds the `EDGE_CURVE`/`ORIENTED_EDGE` args, the `SURFACE_CURVE`/`SEAM_CURVE` unwrapping, conics and points. The boxes and fits use it too, so there's no third resolver.
- ADR-0001 gets a "M1a's realised edge polylines" note (API, sweep rule, the OCCT join, data). There's a CHANGELOG entry and a CI step (`mesh_edges` on nist-pmi + occt-import-js).

## Tests
- `cargo test mesh::edges` (6 unit tests): chord ≤ d (dense sampling, plus analytic on circles) and turn ≤ a against the exact conic. Radii run from 1e-3 to 1e3 mm with d > r, and ellipses go down to B/A = 0.01 (both axis orders). Also covered: `same_sense = .F.`, closed edges, seam crossings both ways, exact §2.6 counts, §2.6's B²/A counter-example, no NaN, and ends bit-equal to the vertex points. Sharing: two faces get identical points in opposite order.
- `tests/mesh_edges.rs`, OCCT as the golden reference. The join goes by geometry, since M5 brings the ids: same ends within d, and each pair within 2·d both ways. Leftovers (healing splits some closed circles in two) are compared point by point against the other side's whole edge set. On `tests/data`, nist-pmi and occt-import-js: **0 unmatched**, 18,684 edges joined 1:1 and 738 polylines compared point by point. Worst gaps are 0.50·d (Rust → OCCT) and 0.998·d (OCCT → Rust). 27 multi-file products are skipped and counted, because their geometry is in other files.
- Local gate: fmt, clippy `-D warnings`, the full `cargo test` and `cargo doc -D warnings` all pass. The graph_bounds and graph_fits corpus gates also pass after the resolver move.

## Data: Ultimaker (`--release`, one thread, `examples/mesh_edges.rs`)
96,561 edges → 80,500 polylines, 253,878 points in ~270 ms. The other 16,061 are B-splines (M1b).
The graph's model diagonal is 42,076 mm against OCCT's 2,572 mm (16×), which is §2.6's "use the OCCT-equivalent diagonal" case. The counts are identical at either d because the 20° bound dominates.
