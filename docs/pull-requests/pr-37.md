# [PR 37](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/37) feat(view): pick a face and inspect its exact surface

## Description

#29: click a face in `stepv view` to pick it on the GPU, and the inspector says what it exactly is, from `--topology`. It also adds the section plane that picking has to honour.

> **Stacked on #26 and #35 (both unmerged).** This branch includes their commits. **Review only the commits after `df586e9`** (#35's head). When those merge, I'll rebase onto main and the diff shrinks.

## Type of Change

- [x] `feat` -- New feature

## Changes Made

- **Id pass** (`src/view/gpu.rs`):
  - It renders `(part + 1, face)` into a single-sample `Rg32Uint` target.
  - It uses the image's own vertex shader, visibility bitset and section plane, so a pick hits exactly what is drawn: nothing hidden, nothing through a cut.
  - Two channels rather than `part << 20 | face`, so there's no 4096-part ceiling.
  - A click copies one texel and maps it asynchronously (`PendingPick::poll`); the result lands on a later frame.
- **Highlight:** a second pass over the picked face's part. It keeps only that face, blends it in the theme accent, and is pulled forward by a negative depth bias with no depth write. Materials are untouched.
- **Inspector** (`src/view/inspect.rs`): rows from the topology, placed into model coordinates by the part's transform:
  - the surface type and its parameters: a plane's normal; a cylinder's radius, ⌀ and axis; a cone's half-angle; a sphere's centre; a torus's two radii;
  - the face's area;
  - the part's volume, size and minimum corner.
- **Selection:** Esc clears it first, then quits.
- **Section plane:** X, Y or Z across the model box, flippable. It applies in every pass: image, ids, highlight, lines. Capping stays in #34.
- **`stepv view`** now asks the kernel for `--topology`. If the topology fails to parse or doesn't match the mesh, the viewer opens anyway, says so, and shows mesh ids.
- **Test hook:** `STEPV_VIEW_PICK=x,y` clicks there once the first frame is drawn, and reports what it hit on stderr. With a screenshot, it waits for the highlight.
