# [PR 173](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/173) feat(graph): G3a, the product structure as a DAG

Slice **G3a** of ADR-0001 (§2.2, §2.4, §6): the product structure as a DAG. It is the first of two stacked PRs (architect's split of G3). **G3b** (placements, units and mapped items) follows on `feature/135-graph-placements` once this merges.

Refs: #135

## What changed
- **`src/graph/structure.rs`**: `graph::structure(&Graph) -> Result<Structure, StructureError>`, O(products + NAUOs), no OCCT. It mirrors `kernel/outline.cpp`:
  - **Roots** follow `STEPControl_Reader::NbRootsForTransfer`: a product definition no NAUO names as its child, documents excluded.
  - **Children** come in the outline's NAUO order.
  - **Shown products:** a product is shown when it has a body (an SDR on its shape, or on a non-GD&T shape aspect) or a shown child; shapeless products are dropped.
  - **Names** follow STEPCAF's rules plus the kernel's (a node takes its product's name, else its instance's).
  - **Leaf counts** are computed bottom-up and saturate.
  - **Cycles:** an iterative three-colour DFS refuses a cycle with `StructureError::Cycle`.
- **Without expanding:** `leaf_path` / `leaf_of` cost O(depth × fan-out). `occurrences(Expand)` is lazy, and `tree(g, Expand)` builds the outline's tree from it.
- **Caps:** both use the outline's caps (depth 60, 1 M nodes) and fail with its exact messages. A child whose subtree reaches past the depth cap is visited even when it isn't shown, since the outline counts every child past depth 60 as shown and fails there.
- **`TextRef` + `Graph::text` / `text_ref`**: owned string handles, so `Structure` holds no borrow of the graph (ADR §2.4).
- **`examples/graph_scan.rs`** also times `structure()`.
- **Docs:** ADR §2.4 records where the realised API differs from the sketch. Edge cases that differ from OCCT outside the corpus are filed as #157.

## Tests (each kernel run under `stepv-cap --memory-mb 4096`, one at a time)
- `nix develop -c cargo test graph::`: exit 0, 28 passed (naming rules, shapeless products and documents, leaf paths, caps, cycle, `TextRef`).
- `nix develop -c cargo test --test graph_structure`: exit 0, 5 passed.
  - A cycle refuses, and the outline fails it too.
  - Past the node cap, a shared sub-assembly (21 products, 2^20 leaves) gives the outline's error, while `structure()` stays under 200 ms.
  - Past the depth cap, a 61-deep chain gives the outline's error, and the 60-deep one equals the outline.
  - `tests/data` equals the outline.
- **Corpus** (`STEPV_CORPUS`, 389 corpus files plus Ultimaker 3): **390/390 trees equal** `occt::outline` (names, shape, leaf numbers), the multi-file assemblies included, since both read only the given file. Every leaf's `leaf_path` → `leaf_of` round-trips.
- **Time** (release, sage): `structure()` takes 0.037 s on Ultimaker (+13.2 MB, mostly `referrers`; 39 B per entity with the table) and 0.036 s on the 221 MB stress file (+28.0 MB; 39 B per entity), inside §2.7.
