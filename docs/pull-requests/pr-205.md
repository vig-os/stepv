# [PR 205](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/205) feat(mesh): M1b, B-spline edge polylines

Fixes #143

## What
- **`src/mesh/edges/bspline.rs`**: `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` edges (rational or not, simple or complex form, directly or through `SURFACE_CURVE`/`SEAM_CURVE`) now go through the single exit `Polylines::build`.
  - De Boor's triangle gives the blossom, and with it each Bézier span over a trimmed range directly.
  - De Casteljau halving continues until the span's control points lie within d of its chord and every two legs turn by at most **a/2**.
  - Spans are put in standard form (equal end weights) before each test.
- **Corners:** C0 knots (interior multiplicity ≥ p) and a closed curve's seam are exact polyline points where the turn bound is exempt. This is documented on `edges` and in ADR §2.6 for M2/M3. Degree 1 comes out as exactly the control polygon.
- **The range:** vertices are located on the curve by branch and bound, with a chord-based lower bound. The edge then runs in the curve's direction, or is reversed exactly for `same_sense = .F.`, wrapping through a closed curve's seam when it must.
- **Resolver `curve::bspline`** (`src/graph/curve.rs`) validates before evaluating. Each failure is routed with its reason:
  - degree (1..=25);
  - multiplicities, and knot count n + p + 1;
  - decreasing or non-finite knots;
  - discontinuous interior knots;
  - empty range;
  - weights finite and > 0, one per control point;
  - control points finite in mm.

  Further routes on the edge: `vertex off curve`, `vertex order`, `closed edge on an open curve`, `zero tangent` (a vanishing derivative at a joint), `too many segments` (48 halvings or 2^20 segments).
- **ADR §2.6 amended** per the architect's decision: the a/2 criterion, its soundness argument, the counterexample to "legs within a", the rejected cross-span alternative, and C0 knots exempt. Also an "M1b's realised" note and a CHANGELOG entry.
- **Golden gate** (`tests/mesh_edges.rs`):
  - OCCT's B-spline face edges are compared too, with M1a's join and bounds.
  - A file that can't be compared now **fails** (#200, #203).
  - OCCT's own healing seams (twice in one face, no `EDGE_CURVE`) and the OCCT edges whose ends match an edge Rust routes are counted, not compared, and only after they fail the comparison.
  - New fixture: a plate bounded by a cubic and a complex-form rational semicircle.

## Tests
- `nix develop -c cargo test mesh::edges`: 21 tests, exit 0. Dense sampling with an independent Cox–de Boor evaluator (adaptive where weights make the curve rush) checks:
  - degree 1;
  - a rational quarter circle at r 1e-3..1e3 (points on the analytic circle, analytic sag ≤ d);
  - a 9-point full circle closed through the seam and wrapped;
  - a cubic with a full-multiplicity kink (the exact corner is present, and the bounds hold on both sides);
  - a double C1 knot;
  - a trimmed range, with `.F.` giving exactly the reversed points;
  - an unclamped closed cubic;
  - weights from 1e-6 to 1e6 (one case is routed `zero tangent`, below f64 resolution);
  - huge coordinates, with nothing non-finite;
  - every malformed-input route;
  - the ADR counterexample.
- **Pre-change:** with the B-spline dispatch disabled, all 9 new tests fail (`unsupported curve`). With "legs within a", the counterexample turns 0.691 rad against a = 0.349.
- **Local gate** (`fmt --check`, `clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test`): exit 0.
- **Golden** on tests/data + nist-pmi + occt-import-js (88 files):
  - 0 unmatched, 0 not compared;
  - 2,111 B-spline polylines;
  - worst Rust→OCCT 0.500·d, OCCT→Rust 1.057·d;
  - counted, not compared: 211 OCCT seams and 12 edges routed in Rust (31 `QUASI_UNIFORM_CURVE` → #204; 1 `zero tangent`).

## Data
