# [PR 154](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/154) feat(graph): G2, references, lazy arguments, complex parts and the census

Slice G2 of ADR-0001 (§2.1, §2.4, §2.6, §2.7, §6).

Fixes #134 and Fixes #149

## What changed
- **Forward CSR in the scan** (`lex.rs` collects each record's `#n`; `Graph::refs`): every reference across all parts, in record order, resolved per reference with `Graph::resolve(id, referrer)`, never `node(id)` (#149). Dangling references are left out of `refs`, listed by `Graph::dangling`, and give `ArgError::Dangling` from `args`.
- **Lazy arguments** (`src/graph/args.rs`): `args(n)` / `args_of(n, part)` → `Args` of `Value` (`Ref`, `Int`, `Real`, `Str(Untrusted)`, `Enum`, `Binary`, `List`, `Typed`, `Null`, `Derived`). Depth capped at 64 (`ArgError::Depth`, never a stack overflow). `args` on a complex entity is `ArgError::Complex`. `Untrusted` has no `Display`, and its `Debug` shows only the length.
- `is_a`, `parts`, `of_type` over complex parts; `referrers` (reverse CSR, counting sort, `OnceLock`); `census()` by type and by part.
- `examples/graph_census.rs`: G2's corpus acceptance (args for every entity and part, refs equal to the arguments' references) plus the fast-path census and the heap with and without `referrers`.
- **Docs:** `docs/adr/0001-census.md` (new) replaces §1's grep table and §2.6's guess. §2.1's duplicate-id bullet amended (#149), the dangling rule made explicit, and §2.4 notes `TypeName::Complex(&[Box<str>])` (#152).
