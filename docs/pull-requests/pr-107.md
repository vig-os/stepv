# [PR 107](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/107) feat(macos): the Quick Look extensions run the kernel out of process (#64 part 2)

## #64 part 2: the Quick Look extensions run the kernel out of process

Closes #64. Builds on #98 (part 1: the supervisor service), #103 (the kernel reads its descriptor directly) and #91.

### What changes
- **No kernel in the extensions.** They link `libstepv_capi.a` only (the renderer and header reader). `otool -L` shows no libstepvocct and no OCCT, and `scripts/test-macos.sh` now checks that. The app's `Frameworks/` serves the CLI's kernel alone.
- **Thumbnail:**
  - The fd goes to the service, which runs the kernel; the extension replies once with the rendered image.
  - **Deadline 15 s**, enforced by the service, plus a 17 s backstop in the extension. Quick Look's thumbnail watchdog is reportedly about 20 s, and a thumbnail has nothing to show partway, so the kernel gets 15 s and the render and reply get the rest.
- **Preview:**
  - The header shows at once, then the assembly tree (the outline, indented, at most 14 lines) and live progress, then the scene.
  - **`handler(nil)`:** once the header and tree are on screen. With no tree (IGES, BREP, a failed or oversized outline), on the first mesh or the failure. At the latest **after 20 s** (the watchdog is about 30 s; 10 s of margin).
  - The view lives on, so the run may take up to **120 s** (the 42 s Ultimaker assembly loads). The run is cancelled when the view goes away.
- **Memory cap:** 4096 MB in both, the CLI's default.

### ql-e2e on the real extensions (`just ql-e2e`)
Every check reads #113's single `log stream` and matches by identity, never by time:
- Each extension logs which run of which service took its file: `accepted: run #N (service pid P), thumbnail|preview <path>`. `scripts/ql-e2e/runs.py` reads that run's own lines and judges by stream order alone. `runs_test.py` checks each verdict on made-up streams first, with a pid-prefix decoy among them.
- A preview and a thumbnail for STEP, IGES and BREP. Each preview must be ready exactly once: on its tree before the scene for STEP, on its mesh for IGES and BREP.
- A 4000-part file is thumbnailed past its 15 s deadline, with `box.brep` beside it.
  - At L ≥ 2, box.brep's run is never queued and exits before the held run's kill.
  - At L = 1, box.brep's run is queued and spawns only after that kill, if at all.
  - Then the held run's `killed (deadline)`, no thumbnail, its kernel gone, and the next thumbnail under 3 s.
- **Closed preview:** the host closes on a signal sent once the file's ready line is in the stream, never at a set time. It logs its close and its quit 3 s later. The kernel's SIGKILL cancel must fall between the two, be reaped within 1 s, and be gone.
- **Closed before ready:** Quick Look keeps the controller until its handler is called, so the kernel is cancelled at the preview's ready (20 s at the latest), not at the close (#122). The case closes on the kernel's spawn. The close must come before the ready line, and the cancel after it, before the quit.
- **Switched preview:** the host switches the view to `box.brep` once the file is ready. The old kernel must be cancelled between the switch and the quit, and box.brep must be shown.
  - Quick Look makes a new controller per item; this was measured again: no controller is prepared twice.
  - For a closed or switched preview, no result line may follow for the file that went, and its handler must complete exactly once.
- **A cancelled session** (closed, or superseded by a second prepare: `StepvPreviewSlot`):
  - Its kernel is killed.
  - Nothing of it reaches the view: a late tree, progress, result, ready or decode is dropped and logged `stale, dropped`.
  - Quick Look's handler completes exactly once: `nil` when ready, `CocoaError(.userCancelled)` if cancelled first. It is then released, because it retains the controller.
- The supervisor probe and the cache control are unchanged from main.

### Results (head 08eb056; sage, macOS 26.4; rebased on main after #113, #81, #116 and #119)
- `just macos-test`: exit 0. That includes the preview-session unit tests, the linkage check and the supervisor probe.
- **Linkage check fails closed:** it requires a Mach header and at least one `(compatibility version` line. A text file fails it, and so does `/nonexistent`. The old check passed a text file, because `otool -L` exits 0 on one.
- `just ql-e2e` on the product build (L = 4): exit 0, **ALL PASS**.
  - Deadline: killed at 15 023 ms, reaped in 22.4 ms, gone.
  - Concurrent: box.brep was never queued and exited before the kill.
  - No throttle: 112 ms.
  - Closed: cancelled between the close and the quit, reaped in 17.7 ms.
  - Switched: the same, reaped in 18.7 ms.
- **The same run on an L = 1 test build** (CI's 7 GB case): exit 0, ALL PASS. box.brep was queued and spawned after huge.step's kill (killed at 15 031 ms).
- **Without the `deinit` cancel** (mutant): FAILURES. The closed and the switched preview were each cancelled only after the host quit.
- **Controller reuse** (round 6, `PreviewSessionTests`): A's result arrives and its decode is still running when B supersedes it.
  - Passes: A shows nothing and its handler completes once, cancelled. B is ready on its own result, and its handler completes once.
  - Without the liveness check: exit 1. A's result is shown, and it marks B ready.
  - Without the handler's release: exit 1. In ql-e2e the closed and the switched previews were then cancelled only at the quit.
- cbbe55e's macos-15 CI was red: the closed preview was cancelled only at the quit. The tree had not come by the fixed 8 s close, and the controller lives until its handler is called. Closes and switches are now ordered on the logged ready (08eb056).
- Round 6 on 08eb056: `just macos-test` exit 0, `just ql-e2e` exit 0, ALL PASS. Closed before ready: ready (tree) at 4.47 s, then the cancel, reaped in 15.9 ms.
- Nits filed: #120 (`.p21`), #121 (a mesh decode error is reported as "no geometry"). Design question: #122 (call the handler sooner?).
- `scripts/ql-e2e/supervisor.sh`: exit 0. `cargo fmt --check`: exit 0. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: exit 0. No kernel change.
