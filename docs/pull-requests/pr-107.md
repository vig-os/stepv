# [PR 107](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/107) feat(macos): the Quick Look extensions run the kernel out of process (#64 part 2)

## #64 part 2: the Quick Look extensions run the kernel out of process

Closes #64. Builds on #98 (part 1: the supervisor service), #103 (the kernel reads its descriptor directly) and #91.

### What changes
- **No kernel in the extensions.** They link `libstepv_capi.a` only (the renderer and header reader). `otool -L` shows no libstepvocct and no OCCT, and `scripts/test-macos.sh` now checks that. The app's `Frameworks/` serves the CLI's kernel alone.
- **Thumbnail:**
  - The fd goes to the service, which runs the kernel; the extension replies once with the rendered image.
  - **Deadline 15 s**, enforced by the service, plus a 17 s backstop in the extension. Quick Look's thumbnail watchdog is reportedly about 20 s, and a thumbnail has nothing to show partway, so the kernel gets 15 s and the render and reply get the rest.
- **Preview:**
  - The header shows at once, then the assembly tree (the outline, indented, at most 14 lines) and live progress, then the scene.
  - **`handler(nil)`:** once the header and tree are on screen. With no tree (IGES, BREP, a failed or oversized outline), on the first mesh or the failure. At the latest **after 20 s** (the watchdog is about 30 s; 10 s of margin).
  - The view lives on, so the run may take up to **120 s** (the 42 s Ultimaker assembly loads). The run is cancelled when the view goes away.
- **Memory cap:** 4096 MB in both, the CLI's default.

### ql-e2e on the real extensions (`just ql-e2e`)
- A preview and a thumbnail for STEP, IGES and BREP. Each preview must log `ready (tree)` before its scene for STEP, and `ready (mesh)` for IGES and BREP, exactly once.
- A 4000-part stress file is thumbnailed past its deadline, with `box.brep` thumbnailed beside it.
  - box.brep must draw while the held run continues.
  - The held one gets no thumbnail, the service logs `killed (deadline)`, and the kernel pid is gone.
  - The next thumbnail must take under 3 s.
- The supervisor probe and the cache control from #98 are unchanged.

### Results (final head; sage, macOS 26.4; rebased on #108)
- **`scripts/test-macos.sh`**: exit 0.
  - The linkage check fails closed: `/nonexistent` and a text file fail it.
  - The preview session's unit tests pass, with a fake service that never replies. Without the backstop they fail (exit 1).
- **`scripts/ql-e2e/run-all.sh` on the product build (L = 16)**: exit 0, **ALL PASS**.
  - Preview ready rule: `ready (tree)` before the scene for STEP; `ready (mesh)` for IGES and BREP.
  - Concurrent: `box.brep` drew while `huge.step` still ran.
  - Deadline: `huge.step` killed at 15 064 ms and reaped in 61 ms; its kernel pid is gone.
  - No throttle: the next thumbnail took 166 ms.
  - **Close:** a preview of the 4000-part file, closed at 8 s, had its kernel cancelled 0.03 s after the host's logged close, reaped in 29.5 ms, and gone. Without the `deinit` cancel it fails: the kill came only when the host quit.
  - **Cache control:** a repeated request is caught as a cached thumbnail.
- **The same run on an L = 1 test build (CI's 7 GB case)**: exit 0, ALL PASS. `box.brep` queued behind `huge.step` and spawned only after `huge.step`'s kill.
- **Backstop:** past 122 s the preview cancels a run whose service never replies, shows why, and drops a late reply.
- `cargo fmt --check && cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: exit 0. No kernel change.
- **Root cause of the earlier red CI** (688855f, `cache control: a cached thumbnail passed`):
  - `log show --start` takes whole seconds and the verdicts matched by file alone. So the repeated request, answered from Quick Look's cache in 7 ms, passed on the previous request's "drawn" line for the same file, in the same second.
  - Every assertion is now scoped to its own request, to the microsecond (`e2elog.py`). Reproduced 5 of 5 before the fix; caught 5 of 5 after.
