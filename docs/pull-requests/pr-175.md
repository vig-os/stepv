# [PR 175](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/175) feat(graph): G3b, placements in mm

Slice **G3b** of ADR-0001 (§2.2): placements in mm, on top of G3a (#173, merged).

Fixes #135

## What changed
- **Instance placements** (`Instance::placement`) follow OCCT's change of coordinates (`STEPControl_ActorRead::ComputeTransformation`, read for behaviour only): `T = S_mm(item_child)⁻¹ · S_mm(item_parent)`, applied to the child's shape.
  - Each item is scaled to mm by its own representation's unit (`STEPConstruct_UnitContext`): an SI prefix, or a conversion-based unit's value times its SI target's prefix.
  - `item_k` goes with `rep_k`, and items found in each other's representation are swapped back.
  - The relationship is inverted when it reverses the NAUO (`CheckSRRReversesNAUO`).
  - Frames are built as `gp_Ax2` builds them, OCCT's default X direction included.
- **Model placements:** `Occurrence::placement` composes the instances along the path.
- **`mapped_placement(g, item, rep)`:** a `MAPPED_ITEM`'s `S_mm(origin)⁻¹ · S_mm(target)`.
- **Unplaced cases:** a cartesian transformation operator, a non-axis item, or a missing relationship is `Unplaced(reason)`, never the identity.
- `Product::mm_per_unit`.

## Friction
A mapped item's move is baked into the kernel's geometry: `--topology` gives the part an identity transform and moves the prototype itself. So the mapped-item test checks a vertex against `T · p` rather than a transform. That is noted in `mapped_placement`'s rustdoc and in the test.
