# [PR 178](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/178) feat(graph): G4, conservative boxes from the graph

Slice G4 of ADR-0001 (§2.3, §2.4, §2.7, §6).

Fixes #136

## What changed
- **`src/graph/bounds.rs`**: `graph::bounds(&Graph, &Structure) -> Bounds`, with `model()`, `product(p)`, `leaf(n)` and `node(n)`, giving `Bound::Box(BBox)` in mm or `Bound::Unknown(Reason)`. No OCCT.
  - §2.3's per-type table: `VERTEX_POINT`; edges on `LINE` (their vertices), `CIRCLE`/`ELLIPSE` (the whole conic), B-splines (control points, every weight > 0), and `SURFACE_CURVE`/`SEAM_CURVE`.
  - Faces on the ruled surfaces use their bounds. A sphere is its whole box plus its bounds. A torus and a surface of revolution use local boxes carried through 8 corners. B-spline surfaces use their control points, and an offset surface its basis grown by |d|.
  - Anything else is `Unknown(type)`.
- **Composition:** one post-order pass over the DAG; each product is boxed once in its own mm, and instances apply their placement through 8 corners. `MAPPED_ITEM`s and AP203 hybrid `SHAPE_REPRESENTATION_RELATIONSHIP`s are walked as OCCT's transfer walks them.
- **`Unknown` propagates:** a node with any `Unknown` child is `Unknown`. Any non-finite coordinate, radius, weight, unit or placement gives `Unknown`, never a box containing NaN (#176). Shared surfaces and curves are boxed once.
- **`examples/graph_scan.rs`** also prints the boxes' time and structure + boxes, §2.7's row.
- **ADR-0001:**
  - §2.3's "Checked against" bullet and the G4 row of §6 now name the new gate, with the reason OCCT's box is no oracle.
  - §2.4 notes G4's realised API.
  - §2.7 records the measured time and its cause.

## The gate: exact geometry, not OCCT's box (architect's decision, round 2)
- OCCT's `BndLib` boxes a conic arc by points at `r / cos(π/8)`, reaching up to about 8.2% of a radius past the exact shape. In round 1, "contains OCCT's box" failed on 234 products by up to 62.8 mm, while no kernel vertex lay outside.
- **`tests/graph_bounds.rs`** now runs the kernel once per file (`--mesh --edges --topology`, under `stepv-cap` at 4 GiB). Every triangulation node, every polyline point of a face edge and every B-rep vertex of a face edge must lie inside the product's graph box, and, placed, inside the leaf's box. The slack is only f32 rounding (1e-6 of size). Free edges (wireframe) are left out, as §2.3 leaves them out of the box.
- It fails when `STEPV_CORPUS` names no file or compares no product. CI runs it on nist-pmi and occt-import-js (`kernel.yml`).
- **Mutation check:** shrinking circle boxes by 10% gives 25 violations on the CI subset.

## Results
- **Local corpus** (390 files with Ultimaker 3): 2,787 products and 3,188 leaves; **9,655,357 points, 0 violations**. Not compared: two ABC files (1,250-hole plates), which hit the kernel's 4 GiB cap.
- **CI subset:** 134 products, 549,005 points, 0 violations.
- **OCCT's box, as data:** it reaches past the graph's box on 561 of 2,713 products, worst 62.8 mm.
- **Tightness (graph ÷ OCCT box volume):** median 1.000, p95 14.2. The tail is §2.3's documented looseness:
  - B-spline control polygons of large untrimmed surfaces (Ultimaker product 167: poles reach 34 m on a ~30 mm part);
  - whole-conic boxes of ellipse arcs with large semi-axes (ABC 00000236);
  - whole-sphere boxes on thin parts (TAIL_TURBINE).
  - Round 1's 4.5e5 p95 on the CI subset was a test bug: one piece of a split product compared against the whole product's box. It is fixed: a product is now compared with the union of its parts' boxes.
- **§2.7 structure + boxes** (release, sage):
  - Ultimaker 0.76 s against ≤ 0.5 s, a **miss**: #177.
  - The stress assembly 0.25 s against ≤ 0.75 s.
  - Cause: random-order memory latency over the 143 MB map. Points parse 3× slower shuffled than in file order. Argument parsing is about 61% of the profile.
