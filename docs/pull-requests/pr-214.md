# [PR 214](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/214) feat(mesh): M3a, cylinder and cone faces

M3a of ADR-0001 §6. This covers cylinder and cone faces, the shared face, seam and pole machinery, and the owner's #210 floor. Spheres and tori are M3b (#212).

## What changed
- **`mesh::analytic`** (new) meshes `ADVANCED_FACE`s on `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE` and `CONICAL_SURFACE`:
  - closed-form (u, v) inversion after the on-surface check;
  - u unwrapped along each loop, so a seam edge becomes the two sides of the cut;
  - the cone's apex as a pole line, each vertex carrying its ruling's normal;
  - exact per-vertex normals;
  - every triangle checked against d and a, else the face routes `rs-fail:deflection`.
- **Virtual seam** (owner decision, now in ADR §2.6):
  - Bands with no seam edge (two circles, a circle plus a `VERTEX_LOOP` apex, or a circle alone on a cone) are cut from A to the nearest-u point B, or to the apex.
  - The seam adds no point on any edge, and its two copies share one set of 3-D points.
  - A seam across a hole routes `loop:seam-crosses` (35 faces on ABC).
- **Steiner points on developable surfaces:**
  - u is resolved at half the circle step.
  - The straight rulings take no grid row. On nist a full grid made 447 apex folds, and on Ultimaker 32.7 M triangles in 197 s.
  - Where a triangle breaks the bound, its centroid is added, for up to 16 rounds.
- **`mesh::face`** (new) is shared with `mesh::plane`. It holds `Context`, `FaceMesh` (now with per-vertex normals), `Route`, the walk, the CDT, the flood and the orientation check.
- **#210 floor:** `Context::of` applies `max(declared, 1e-7 mm)` once and records `uncertainty:floored`.
- **Units:** a cone's semi-angle is read in the file's plane angle unit (69 corpus files use degrees).
- **ADR §2.6** now records:
  - the floor;
  - the virtual-seam rule and why it adds no edge point;
  - that developable surfaces keep the chord bound;
  - the doubly curved boundary bound, at most about 4/3·d, with its proof (for M3b).
- **Golden:** `tests/mesh_plane.rs` is now `tests/mesh_faces.rs`, comparing planar and cylinder/cone faces. The CI step is renamed to match.

## Tests
`nix develop -c cargo test mesh::analytic`:
- watertight once seam and pole duplicates are identified, area within the bound, triangles within d (sampled) and a;
- full, inward-facing and partial cylinders, and a seamless two-circle band;
- cones down to the apex: with a seam edge, with a `VERTEX_LOOP`, and implicit;
- the cylinder–cylinder intersection along a rational B-spline, with and without a seam edge;
- routing with reasons, the #210 floor, and degrees.
