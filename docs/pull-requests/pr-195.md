# [PR 195](https://github.com/vig-os/stepv/pull/195) feat(view): G6c, primitive fits

Fixes #140

ADR-0001 §2.8 and the G6c row of §6, on top of G6b (#192).

## What changed
- **Fits** (`src/graph/bounds/fit.rs`): `graph::fits(&g, &bounds)` fits each product with a known box and no shown child.
  - Cylinder: every face is on a coaxial cylinder, cone or torus, or a perpendicular plane.
  - Sphere: every face is on one sphere, or on a plane.
  - The radius and axial extent follow §2.8's per-surface bounds, inflated by ε = the axes' (or centres') largest distance + the context's length uncertainty. A fit is refused past 10× the uncertainty.
  - `Fits::leaf` places the fit like the box and keeps it only when its **drawn** volume is smaller than the leaf's box. Drawn means a circumscribed 32-gon prism, or a 12×24 lat-long polyhedron at r / cos α.
- **One curve walker**: the box's curve evaluation in `bounds.rs` now yields pieces (points and whole conics), and the box is their union. Fits read the same pieces. The box itself is unchanged, and `graph_bounds` still passes.
- **Viewer**: `Placeholders::fits` and `shapes()`; `GpuPlaceholders::upload` takes `Shape`s (a box or a fit), drawn only by the placeholder pipelines. The status line now reads `graph: 3 parts, 3 shown as placeholders` (boxes and fits together).
- **Readings of §2.8 it doesn't spell out** (recorded in the ADR's "G6c's realised fits"):
  - The fitted axis is the first non-planar face's.
  - A plane counts as perpendicular when its edges span at most 10× the uncertainty along the axis.
  - A context without a length uncertainty keeps the box.
- **Tests**: `tests/graph_fits.rs` covers:
  - a hand-written ball (sphere);
  - the bracket's pin (shaft, cylinder) and plate (box fallback);
  - axes 1e-5 mm apart at 1e-7 uncertainty (rejected; 5e-7 accepted with ε = 6e-7);
  - the drawn-vs-inscribed size case;
  - circumscription of the drawn shapes;
  - the corpus gate.

  The kernel-oracle helpers moved from `graph_bounds.rs` to `tests/corpus/`. CI runs the gate in the Kernel job.

## Results
- Local corpus (`STEPV_CORPUS`, 389 files): **345 fits (314 cylinders, 31 spheres) on 2,611 leaves with a box (13.2%), 264,962 kernel points, 0 outside.**
- Local, exit codes:
  - `cargo test --test graph_fits` 0
  - `--test view` 0
  - `cargo test graph::` 0
  - `--test cache` 0
  - `--test graph_bounds` 0
  - fmt + clippy `-D warnings` 0
- Shown to fail without the change: with `fits()` returning no fits, 4 of the 6 `graph_fits` tests fail (`pin 1: None`, …), and so does `fits_are_drawn_as_placeholders_never_shaded`.

## Size
About 605 net Rust product lines, not counting rustdoc and blank lines: about 705 added (`fit.rs` is 517) and about 100 removed by the bounds refactor. That is over the ~400 guide and under ~650.
